## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
OS.ID-Booking is the official OrangeSky app for booking hotel rooms and claiming vouchers right from your phone.

Lightweight, fast to open, and built for OrangeSky guests:

• Home — browse OrangeSky properties and the latest offers.
• Voucher — search, sort, and claim current promotions.
• Help — reach the OrangeSky team by phone, email, or WhatsApp.
• Booking — open and track your reservations.
• Account — sign in and manage your OrangeSky account.

The main navigation is a floating pill at the bottom of the screen, so switching menus takes one tap. Vouchers can be searched and sorted, terms and conditions are collapsed for quick scanning, and "Load more" keeps the page light. Each tab remembers its scroll position, so returning to Voucher does not start from the top.

Pull to refresh, pinch to zoom, and text follows your phone's font size setting. Prices stay consistent (Rp690,000) and dates use the Indonesian format (9 Nov 2025).

The app runs on Android 8.0 and above, with no ads, no tracking, and only the internet permission.
```
